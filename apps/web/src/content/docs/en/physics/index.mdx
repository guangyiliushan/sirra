Physics is the most fundamental of the natural sciences. It seeks to understand how the universe behaves — from the motion of galaxies to the interactions of subatomic particles. This knowledge base collects notes, derivations, and references across its major branches.

*To glimpse the quiet logic that binds the universe, to trace the threads of cause and effect woven into every corner of space and time — that is the physicist's quiet poetry: to understand not just what is, but what might be, to reach across the chasm of the unknown and touch the shape of things yet to come.*
